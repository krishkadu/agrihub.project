High-Value Polyhouse Crops & Economics

Protected cultivation yields 3x–5x more than open fields with guaranteed quality and premium mandi pricing

Dutch Roses

Cut Flowers • Export Grade

Top export variety: Top Secret, Taj Mahal, Gold Strike. Requires 15–28°C and 65% humidity.

  • Yield: 220–250 stems / sq.m / year
  • Price: ₹3.50 – ₹12.00 / stem
  • Bed Spacing: 40cm x 15cm on raised beds
Avg. Profit: ₹2.5L – ₹4L / 1,000 sq.m
🫑

Coloured Capsicum

Red & Yellow Bell Pepper

Varieties: Bachata (Yellow), Inspiration (Red). High demand in urban hotels, supermarkets, and export.

  • Yield: 10 – 14 kg / sq.m / year
  • Price: ₹45 – ₹110 / kg
  • Duration: 9–10 months continuous harvest
Avg. Profit: ₹2L – ₹3.2L / 1,000 sq.m
🥒

Parthenocarpic Cucumber

Seedless English Cucumber

Varieties: Hilton, Kian, Silyon. Fast crop cycle allowing 3 rotations per year in polyhouse.

  • Yield: 25 – 35 kg / sq.m / year (3 cycles)
  • Price: ₹25 – ₹55 / kg
  • First Harvest: 35–40 days after transplanting
Avg. Profit: ₹1.8L – ₹2.8L / 1,000 sq.m
🌸

Gerbera Flowers

Floriculture • Year-Round

Top varieties: Dana Ellen, Silvester, Goliath. Highly resilient flower crop with continuous 3-year economic lifespan.

  • Yield: 200 – 240 flowers / sq.m / year
  • Price: ₹2.50 – ₹8.00 / flower
  • Plant Life: 30–36 months productive life

Microclimate Control Parameters

Scientific temperature, humidity, shading, and fertigation targets for maximum polyhouse productivity

Temperature Range

Day: 24°C – 28°C
Night: 16°C – 18°C
Keep side curtains open when ambient temp exceeds 30°C.

Relative Humidity (RH)

Optimal: 60% – 70%
High RH Warning: >85% triggers powdery mildew.
Use overhead 4-way micro foggers (operating at 4 kg/cm² pressure).

Shading Net Factor

Summer: 50% Green/Aluminet thermal net.
Winter / Monsoon: Roll net back for maximum sunlight.
Polyfilm must be 200-micron UV-stabilized 5-layer diffused.

Soil EC & pH Norms

Soil pH: 6.0 – 6.8 (Slightly acidic)
Electrical Conductivity (EC): < 1.0 mS/cm
Perform soil solarization or Hydrogen Peroxide sterilization before planting.

Step-by-Step Government Subsidy Application Guide

Official procedure to apply for MIDH / NHB polyhouse capital subsidy through State Horticulture Portals

1. Land Record & Soil/Water Test Verification

Ensure land title is clear with 7/12 extract and 8A (or regional Record of Rights). Conduct water testing for salinity (EC < 1.0, pH 6.5–7.5, Chloride < 150 ppm).

2. Obtain Quotation & DPR from Accredited Fabricator

Contact an empaneled polyhouse manufacturer (must comply with BIS standard IS 14462:1997). Get a Detailed Project Report (DPR) with structural drawings, cost estimate, and bills of materials.

3. Online Application on Horticulture Portal

Apply through your state's online DBT portal (e.g., MahaDBT in Maharashtra, Hortnet nationally, or NHB portal). Upload Aadhaar, 7/12, bank passbook, quotation, and DPR.

4. Issuance of Pre-Sanction Letter & Bank Term Loan

District Horticulture Officer (DHO) reviews application and issues an official Pre-Sanction Letter. Use this letter to obtain a bank term loan for the non-subsidy component under Priority Sector Lending.

5. Construction & Geotagged Physical Inspection

Erect the polyhouse structure within the sanctioned timeline. Officers perform GPS geotagged photo inspection and verify BIS grade GI pipes (2mm wall thickness) and 200µ polythene sheet.

6. Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Subsidy Disbursement

Subsidy amount (50%–75%) is directly credited to the farmer's bank loan account, drastically reducing interest liability and principle burden.
