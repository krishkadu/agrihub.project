Why AgriHub?

Built for India's 146 Million Farmers

We identified 5 critical pain points and built targeted solutions.

The Problem

  • Information asymmetry in agriculture
  • Incorrect fertilizer usage wastes money
  • Most farmers miss eligible govt schemes
  • Weather unpredictability causes crop loss
  • Difficulty choosing profitable crops

Our Objective

  • One-stop platform for agricultural info
  • Scientific, data-driven recommendations
  • Easy access to 15+ government programs
  • Real-time weather for smart planning
  • AI-powered crop profit optimization

The Impact

  • ₹5,000 avg. savings per farmer/season
  • 30% reduction in fertilizer waste
  • Direct access to ₹6,000 PM-Kisan benefit
  • Avoid weather-related crop loss
  • 40% higher profit with right crop choice
