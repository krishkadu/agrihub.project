Toxicity Classification

Understand Insecticide Color Band Warnings

Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) mandatory color coding

Red Band

Extremely Toxic

Signal Word: POISON (विष)

Oral LD50: 1 - 50 mg/kg

Requires full chemical suit, respirator & face shield. Strictly restricted handling.

Yellow Band

Highly Toxic

Signal Word: DANGER (खतरा)

Oral LD50: 51 - 500 mg/kg

High precaution required. N95 respirator, nitrile gloves & goggles mandatory.

Blue Band

Moderately Toxic

Signal Word: WARNING (चेतावनी)

Oral LD50: 501 - 5000 mg/kg

Avoid contact with skin or eyes. Wear gloves, apron, and protective face mask.

Green Band

Slightly Toxic

Signal Word: CAUTION (सावधानी)

Oral LD50: > 5000 mg/kg

Low hazard under recommended dosages. Basic gloves and wash after handling.

Safety Audit

Pre-Spraying Farmer Safety Checklist

Complete these mandatory safety checks before entering the field with spray equipment

⚠️ 5 Precaution(s) Remaining! Please verify all safety checks before spraying chemical solutions.

Face respirator/mask, chemical-resistant rubber gloves, eye goggles, and long-sleeved shirt/apron equipped.

Do NOT spray during strong winds (>15 km/h), extreme midday sun (>35°C), or impending rainfall. Always spray with the wind at your back.

Check knapsack sprayer pump, hose, and nozzle tips for leaks using clean water before mixing chemicals.

Keep children, pregnant women, and livestock away from the field. Post red flag or warning sign at field entrance.

Confirm days remaining before crop harvest to ensure zero chemical residue on harvested produce.

Standard Operating Procedure

5 Steps to Safe Pesticide Management

Step 01

Storage & Transport

Keep in original containers under lock and key. Store away from food, animal feed, children, and direct sunlight.

Step 02

Mixing & Dilution

Mix chemicals outdoors or in well-ventilated areas. Use a clean stick to stir — NEVER use bare hands.

Step 03

Field Application

Spray smoothly maintaining correct height (50 cm above canopy). Do NOT eat, drink, or smoke while spraying.

Step 04

Pre-Harvest Waiting

Strictly observe the Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) days before plucking crops to avoid toxic pesticide residues.

Step 05

Disposal & Clean-up

Triple-rinse empty chemical containers, puncture them, and bury in dedicated disposal pits away from water sources.
